**Quy ước tham chiếu:**

- `FR-01`, `FR-02`, and `FR-03` are explicit functional requirements from the repository `README.md`.
- `IA-01`–`IA-04` are interface aspects defined by `docs/2026.HW03.md` and the README GUI requirements.
- `REC-*` items are additional usability/accessibility recommendations, not explicit EShop requirements. They are included as recommendations and must not be reported as requirement failures without human review.
